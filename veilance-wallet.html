Seque Card Wallet

A wallet that gets better with every day you carry it. Technical materials meet thoughtful design in something that feels inevitable once you hold it. The leather molds to your cards over time, creating this perfect clam-like fit that feels custom-made. I love how it pairs with my iPhone - the rounded corners align perfectly, making them feel like they were designed together. Veilance removed everything unnecessary, even the stitches, leaving only what serves a purpose. It's the kind of object that makes you appreciate good design every time you reach for it.
Even the stitches were removed. It's pure function without ornament - the kind of restraint that makes everyday objects feel special.
No logos, no flash. Just technical materials and thoughtful engineering that speaks to those who recognize good design. It doesn't need to announce itself.
Gets Better With Time
The leather learns from your cards, creating this perfect clam-like enclosure. After months of use, it feels custom-made for exactly what you carry. Incredibly satisfying to touch.
Perfect iPhone Companion
The rounded corners align perfectly with my iPhone's edges. They feel like they were designed to be held together - two objects that become one in your hand.
