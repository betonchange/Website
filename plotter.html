Notebook System

Brand
Plotter
Designer
Plotter Team
Country
Japan
Year
2017
I've gone back and forth on planners over the years. Digital is practical in its flexibility, but you can't beat paper for the power of thinking through writing and looking back at your thoughts. The Plotter system is optimized for thinking - giving you the constraint of paper and size, but also the flexibility to build your own planner. I love how the leather ages with use, becoming this personal artifact of your thinking. The Japanese understand constraint as a creative force, and this system embodies that philosophy perfectly.
A system that gives structure so you can freely think - helps you organize, reorganize physical artifact of your thoughts.
From Italian Pueblo leather with dynamic aging to Japanese vegetable-tanned varieties - each leather type offers a unique patina journey.
Fully customizable with memo pad refills and accessories for a personalized system. Build your perfect creative tool by mixing paper types, dividers, and accessories to match your exact workflow and thinking style.
The project system - an ingenious idea that helps keeps thoughts together physically.
